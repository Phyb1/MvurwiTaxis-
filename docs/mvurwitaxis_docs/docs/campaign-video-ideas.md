# Campaign Video Ideas

Vertical, short (under 45s), captions on, real screen recordings and
real drivers where possible. Corrected from the earlier draft: Video 2
below used to show "taps WhatsApp" as the main flow — it now shows the
in-platform Message flow, which is what's actually live.

## Video 1 — 5 to 20
**Hook (on screen):** "We're at 5. We're building to 20."

Show real driver profiles scrolling past.

**Voiceover:** "MvurwiTaxis is building its first local taxi network.
Five drivers are already listed. We're looking for the next fifteen."

**End card:** "Are you a Mvurwi taxi driver? DM JOIN."

---

## Video 2 — What a passenger actually does
**Hook:** Homepage loading.

**Show, in order:** Homepage → browse profiles → tap a driver's card →
tap **Message** → confirmation that it's sent.

**Voiceover:** "A passenger finds a taxi, opens the profile, and taps
Message. It goes straight to the driver — no phone number needed
until they reply."

**End card:** "Get your taxi listed. DM JOIN."

---

## Video 3 — Stop reposting the same advert
**Open:** A mock "Taxi available, call/app" style post, the kind drivers
already post daily in groups.

**Text on screen:** "Posting this every day?"

**Cut to:** A real MvurwiTaxis driver profile.

**Text on screen:** "Put it online once. Passengers message you
straight through it."

**End card:** "MvurwiTaxis — DM JOIN."

---

## Video 4 — How a lead actually works
**Show:** Dashboard notification arriving → tapping to unlock → the
tab page.

**Voiceover:** "A passenger messages you, you get alerted instantly.
Inside your free leads, it's free. Past that, twenty cents goes on your
tab — no payment step, you call them right away either way."

**End card:** "Free to start. mvurwitaxis.co.zw/signup/"

---

## Video 5 — Meet a driver
Film a real driver, ask on camera, don't script the answer:
1. "What's your name, and what do you drive?"
2. "Why did you join MvurwiTaxis?"
3. "Did you get a real passenger from it?"

Keep it rough and honest — a slightly unpolished real answer
outperforms a scripted one here.

---

## Video 6 — Build in public
**Show:** The admin dashboard or the driver directory, count visible.

**Text on screen:** "5 drivers → [X] drivers"

**Voiceover:** "We're not claiming a big network. We're showing you the
real one, as it grows."

**End card:** "Want to be counted? DM JOIN."

---

## Video 7 — Request Any Taxi, live
**Show:** Passenger taps "Request Any Taxi" → notification hitting a
driver's phone → driver accepting.

**Voiceover:** "Not sure which driver to pick? Request Any Taxi sends
it to everyone online at once. First to respond gets it."

**End card:** "Go online and catch requests like this. DM JOIN."

## Delivery notes

- Reuse the same homepage/profile screen-recording footage across
  videos where possible — cuts filming time to just the new bit each
  time.
- Keep spoken lines under ~4 seconds each — matches fast cuts and holds
  attention on Status/Reels-length video.
- For Video 5 (driver testimonial), film with the driver centered so it
  crops cleanly to vertical without losing them from frame.
