# Driver Onboarding

The script to walk a new driver through, start to finish — whether
that's a DM reply, a call, or in person. Every step below matches what
they'll actually see on the live site, not an old version of it.

## Before you start

Have these ready to grab from a driver in under a minute:
- Full name (as they'd like passengers to see it)
- Phone number (becomes their login username, never shown to passengers directly)
- Email address (required — see "Why email matters" below)
- Vehicle details, seats, and a photo if they have one handy

## The pitch (30 seconds)

> "MvurwiTaxis is a local directory — passengers browse taxi profiles
> and message you straight through the site. It's free to list, you
> start with 3 free leads a month, and there's no payment step in the
> moment when a lead comes in — that gets settled later, on your own
> time. Takes about 2 minutes to sign up."

## Signup (do this with them, not for them)

1. Go to `mvurwitaxis.co.zw/signup/`.
2. Fill in: full name, phone number, **email**, vehicle details.
3. Explain the free tier as they fill it in: *"Free tier: $0, listed at
   the bottom, 3 free leads a month. You can go Pro any time — $3/week
   or $10/month — for unlimited free unlocks and top placement. No
   pressure to decide that now."*

### Why email matters — say this explicitly

Some drivers will ask why it's required. The honest answer: *"It's how
you get alerted the instant a passenger messages you, and how you'd
reset your password if you forget it."* If you're helping someone who
signed up before email was required, add one from their dashboard
before moving on — nothing past this point works reliably without it.

## First login — what to show them, in this order

1. **The dashboard.** Point out where leads will appear.
2. **GO ONLINE.** *"This is the one habit that matters most. Tap it
   when you're free to take a trip — you'll show as Available for 30
   minutes, and it refreshes every time you open the dashboard. A
   driver who never goes online gets zero leads, full stop."*
3. **Turn on request notifications.** *"Tap this so you get a push
   alert the instant someone messages you, on top of the email."* If
   they're on iPhone: *"Add MvurwiTaxis to your home screen first —
   Share, then Add to Home Screen — then turn notifications on from
   there. It won't work directly from Safari."*
4. **The Tab page.** Explain it now, even though it's empty: *"When
   someone messages you and you unlock their number, if you're within
   your free leads or you're Pro, it's free. After that, 20 cents gets
   added here instead of asking you to pay on the spot — you can call
   the passenger immediately either way. Settle it whenever suits you
   from this page; it just has to be settled once it reaches a dollar
   or hits 7 days old, or new unlocks pause until you do."*
5. **How to pay, when they're ready to settle or go Pro**: EcoCash to
   the number shown on the Tab page, then submit the reference or a
   screenshot. Paynow works too, where enabled. *"This is only for
   settling your tab or upgrading — never for unlocking a lead itself."*

## What actually happens when a passenger messages them — walk through this live if you can

1. A passenger browses the directory or uses "Request Any Taxi," and taps
   **Message** on a driver's card (not WhatsApp — that's the backup
   option on the profile, not the main flow now).
2. The driver gets a push notification and an email with the trip
   details and any note — **not** the passenger's number yet.
3. If it's a free lead (within quota or Pro), the driver unlocks it for
   free and can call/WhatsApp the passenger directly. If it's past
   free quota, unlocking adds 20 cents to their tab — no payment step,
   no waiting on admin, they can call immediately.
4. The passenger gets a private link that updates itself, so they're
   not left guessing — if the driver doesn't respond, it offers them
   WhatsApp or another driver.

Make sure a new driver understands: **responding fast matters more here
than almost anything else** — a passenger with an unanswered request is
one tap away from being offered a different driver.

## Verification — set expectations up front

*"Once you're set up, send a photo of your license and vehicle
registration and I'll verify you — verified drivers rank higher and
passengers trust the listing more. Takes a few minutes once I have the
documents."*

## Close every onboarding with this

> "Three things matter more than anything else: go online when you're
> free, respond fast when a lead comes in, and keep your tab under
> control. Everything else is details."

## After they're signed up

- Send the welcome/onboarding email if it didn't fire automatically —
  check their dashboard shows it as sent.
- Add them to `campaign-tracker.md` the same day, not at the end of the
  week.
- Ask, once they've taken their first lead, who else they know — this
  is the cheapest referral moment there is.
