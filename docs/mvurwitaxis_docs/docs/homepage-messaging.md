# Homepage Messaging

## What's already shipped

The original recommendation in this area was adopted — good news, no
action needed on the core hero:

- **Headline:** "Find a Taxi in Mvurwi" ✅
- **Supporting text:** "Browse local taxi drivers, check availability,
  and contact a driver directly." ✅
- **CTA:** "Request Any Taxi →" ✅
- **Driver CTA:** "Are you a taxi driver in Mvurwi? List your taxi" ✅
- **List Your Taxi page** already states the free tier and Pro pricing
  clearly and accurately: *"Free tier: listed at the bottom, 3
  leads/month. Upgrade to Pro anytime for unlimited leads and top
  placement."* — this matches live pricing exactly, no changes needed.

## One phrase worth tightening in the next pass

"Contact a driver directly" is the one piece of homepage copy that
predates in-platform messaging and hasn't caught up to it. It's not
wrong, but it's ambiguous — a first-time visitor could read "directly"
as "call them" or "WhatsApp them," when the actual flow is tap
Message → alert sent → private status link. Worth testing:

> Browse local taxi drivers, check availability, and message a driver
> straight through the site.

Small change, but it removes the one moment where the homepage's
language and the product's actual behavior could diverge in a new
visitor's head.

## Other small, low-risk additions worth testing

- **Near the Install App button:** a one-line reason to bother — e.g.
  "Add it to your home screen — opens like an app, works offline for
  browsing." Right now "Install App" has no supporting copy telling a
  passenger why they'd want to.
- **On the FAQ page**, the first answer ("How do I book a taxi?")
  already correctly describes the Message-first flow with WhatsApp as
  a fallback — this is the best-written explanation of the product
  anywhere in the docs or the site. Worth mining that exact phrasing
  for campaign copy rather than redrafting it from scratch, since it's
  already accurate and already tested against real user questions.
