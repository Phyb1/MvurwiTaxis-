MvurwiTaxis — Daily Checklist (4-driver stage)
You're still in Phase 1 — target is 15–20 listed drivers, 5+ Verified, before any passenger-facing push. At 4 drivers, most of the day should still be acquisition, not admin. Roughly 5–10 minutes of ops, the rest on getting to driver #5 and beyond.

Every day
Acquisition (the actual priority right now)

 Scan WhatsApp groups for new "taxi available" posts → DM using a template (Template A/B/C)
 Follow up on any warm lead that's gone quiet 2+ days — call instead of text
 Ask each of your 4 signed-up drivers: "know anyone else who'd want in?" — don't wait for it to come up
 Log any new lead's source (WhatsApp text / call / referral / flyer) — you'll want this once you have enough data to see what's converting
Admin (5–10 min, morning or evening)

 Open Admin → Leads & Payments Dashboard
 Confirm any pending payments — cross-check EcoCash reference against your SMS/statement before confirming
 Check for any hot lead open 30+ min with no driver claiming it — WhatsApp your online drivers directly if so
 Remind any driver who isn't marked "online" to toggle it on — a driver who's never online gets zero WhatsApp clicks
Weekly
 Verify any driver who signed up this week but isn't yet marked Verified (license/reg check)
 Skim reviews (once you have any) — anything under 3 stars, reach out to that driver directly
 Sanity-check the fare board still matches what drivers are actually quoting
 Look at "went online in last 7 days" vs total signed-up — this is the number that matters more than the raw driver count
Trigger checks — not daily tasks, just don't forget these thresholds
15–20 drivers, 5+ Verified → safe to start Phase 2 (passenger-facing WhatsApp posts, fare-board content, video posting)
Leads sitting unclaimed even with drivers online → that's a passenger-awareness gap, not a driver-supply gap — don't respond by recruiting harder
Free-tier drivers routinely hitting their lead cap and still not upgrading → the only real signal to reconsider pricing, not before
Skip for now (per growth.md — revisit later, not today)
Paynow automation — not worth it until manual EcoCash confirmation is actually a bottleneck
SMS notifications — WhatsApp + email covers it
Expansion beyond Mvurwi — not until 30+ active drivers here and organic "Request Any Taxi" use
