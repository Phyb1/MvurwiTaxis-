MvurwiTaxis — Marketing Templates
Two audiences, two jobs. Don't mix them: drivers need to hear "free leads, no risk." Passengers need to hear "instant answer, real prices" — the code (search, fare board, "Request Any Taxi") is the proof, so let them tap it rather than explaining it.

1. DM replies to drivers posting "Taxi available" in groups
You've seen posts like:

🚖 Taxi available 24/7. Chero nguva tinouya. Cheap & reliable. Call/app 0778011934

They're already doing free marketing for themselves in every group they can find — that's the itch MvurwiTaxis scratches. Reply in their DMs (not the group), mirror their energy, keep it under 3 lines.

Template A — mirrors their own post back at them

Hi, it's Phyb from MvurwiTaxis. Saw your "available 24/7" post 👀 That's exactly what MvurwiTaxis is for — one link, always up, so you're not retyping that message in every group every day. Free to list, 2 minutes. Join here: https://www.mvurwitaxis.co.zw/signup/

Template B — leads-not-ads framing

Hi, it's Phyb from MvurwiTaxis. Passengers search here and message drivers straight on WhatsApp — like your post, but it never scrolls away. Free listing, you only pay 20c if we send you a specific passenger. Sign up here: https://www.mvurwitaxis.co.zw/signup/

Template C — short, for drivers who don't reply to long texts

Hi, it's Phyb from MvurwiTaxis. Free taxi listing for Mvurwi drivers, more customers, no catch. https://www.mvurwitaxis.co.zw/signup/

Follow-up if they haven't signed up (send once, after ~2 days — no repeats):

Hi, it's Phyb again — link's here whenever you're ready, still free: https://www.mvurwitaxis.co.zw/signup/

2. WhatsApp group posts (passenger-side awareness)
Rule from the marketing plan: one post per group, not a campaign. Rotate these rather than repeating the same one — pick whichever fits the group's vibe (community / church / vendor / school-parent).

Post 1 — the "will they see you" hook (driver-recruiting, for groups with drivers in them)

Hi, it's Phyb from MvurwiTaxis. MvurwiTaxis is coming — a free directory connecting passengers with taxis. Soon passengers will open this and see "Taxis available in Mvurwi" — will they see YOU? Drivers join free: https://www.mvurwitaxis.co.zw/signup/

Post 2 — fare board as proof (passenger-facing, reusable indefinitely)

What's the real price to Harare? 🤔 No more guessing at the rank — MvurwiTaxis has a live fare board. [screenshot] mvurwitaxis.co.zw

Post 3 — "request any taxi" as the value prop

Tired of standing at the rank waiting? On MvurwiTaxis, tap "Request Any Taxi" and every available driver gets notified at once. First to reply gets you. mvurwitaxis.co.zw

Post 4 — social proof once you have Verified drivers (use after Phase 1)

Hi, it's Phyb from MvurwiTaxis. [X] verified Mvurwi taxi drivers are already on MvurwiTaxis — real names, real cars, checked in person. Passengers: search, tap WhatsApp, go — no account needed. mvurwitaxis.co.zw Drivers not listed yet: https://www.mvurwitaxis.co.zw/signup/

How to sequence this (from your own marketing plan)
Don't post passenger-side content (section 2) until 15–20 drivers are listed. An empty directory kills trust on first click — this is the single biggest risk to the whole push.
DM drivers (section 1) this week — every "taxi available" post in a group is a warm lead, not a cold one; they're already advertising, you're offering them a better channel for the same thing.
Once 5+ drivers are Verified, switch to Post 4 — verified names are what gets the next 3 drivers to sign up without a DM at all.
Reuse Post 2 (fare board) whenever the "what's the real price" argument comes up organically in a group — it's evergreen, post it as many times as that conversation happens, just not as a repeated broadcast.
What to skip
No paid ads yet — Mvurwi's market is small enough that DMs + groups + the rank itself cover it.
No discounting the 20c hot-lead price to "grow faster" — the bottleneck right now is awareness, not price resistance.
